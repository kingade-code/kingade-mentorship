Everything you need to know before purchasing.

What brokers work with the bot?

Any broker that supports MetaTrader 5. We use Exness, but IC Markets, XM, Pepperstone, and others work perfectly.

What's the minimum deposit?

We recommend $500 for optimal risk management, but you can start with as little as $100. The bot adjusts lot sizes based on your balance.

How do I install the bot?

After purchase, you'll receive a download link. Extract the files, copy them to your MT5 folder, run the bot, and enter your license key. Setup takes about 5 minutes.

Do I need to be online 24/7?

Yes, MT5 must be running for the bot to trade. We recommend using a VPS (Virtual Private Server) for uninterrupted trading. We can help you set this up.

How does the license key work?

Each license key is tied to one MT5 account. After purchase, you enter your key once and it's saved automatically. Monthly keys expire after 30 days, annual after 365 days, and lifetime keys never expire.

Can I use the bot on multiple accounts?

Each license key works on one MT5 account. If you need multiple accounts, contact us for a discount on bulk purchases.

What instruments does the bot trade?

The bot trades XAUUSD (Gold) on M1 timeframe and GBPUSD & AUDUSD on M15 timeframe. These are the most liquid and profitable instruments for scalping.
